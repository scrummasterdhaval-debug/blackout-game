A 3-minute bluffing game for 2–8 people, on everyone’s own phone.

How it works

  1. 01Five bulbs, worth 1 to 5 points. Every round, everyone secretly taps one.
  2. 02Alone on a bulb? You bank its points.
  3. 03Two or more on the same bulb? It blacks out for good and nobody scores.
  4. 04Every blackout pushes power into what’s left — surviving bulbs gain +1 each time.
  5. 05One Surge per game: if you collide, you take the bulb and it survives. Surge alone and you score nothing.
  6. 06It ends when one bulb is left standing. Most points wins.
